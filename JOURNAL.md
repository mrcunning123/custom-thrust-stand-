## 11/9/2025 - made cad model of the rocket thrust test stand  

Hi i always wanted to make a rocket motor and **thrust test stand.**
So this is the thrust test stand that i designed on [onshape](https://www.onshape.com/en/).  it mainly uses aluminum profile channels and "[Phaeton Load Cell Sensor-CZL-E Weighing Pressed down](https://robu.in/product/phaeton-load-cell-sensor-czl-e-weighing-pressed-down/)" load cells. I will be using[ HX711](https://robu.in/product/smartelex-load-cell-amplifier-hx711/) load cell amplifiers. This test stand  will have a maximum capacity of 1,962newton force.
You can find my design[ here](https://cad.onshape.com/documents/9b6ce8c810e1571b29765428/w/e7dd922261e5498be26276db/e/43a98013dbc41b1c1c47b144) the design is not complete as it need few more electronics to read the data and some support structure for holding the motor in position without tilting .I will be also making a high pressure water rocket motor with glass fiber in the future. You can find my designs [here](https://cad.onshape.com/documents/9b6ce8c810e1571b29765428/w/e7dd922261e5498be26276db/e/43a98013dbc41b1c1c47b144).designing the aluminum profile channels were a very hard task on onshape as it lagged and took almost 2 gb of ram, especially when it was the aluminum 2060.
